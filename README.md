**CORAL BAY** — an open-world getaway game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from
static files with three.js. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car:
traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who
chase and ram you, three story missions for Dana Cruz the getaway driver, combat,
a HUD with a live minimap, a start screen and a save that lives on your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Play with keyboard and mouse: WASD to walk and drive, mouse drag for the camera,
E to enter or steal a car, Space to jump or handbrake, F to punch, Esc to pause.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
